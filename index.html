iGenics®  Ingredients


iGenics® is marketed as an eye-health dietary supplement containing vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and botanical extracts intended to support normal vision and overall eye wellness. The ingredient list can differ between websites and product versions, so verify the current Supplement Facts label before publishing exact amounts.

🍁

Lutein

Lutein is a carotenoid antioxidant found naturally in the retina, particularly in the macula. It helps support macular health and protects eye tissues from oxidative stress. Lutein is commonly included in eye-health supplements to complement normal visual function and long-term eye wellness.
🍁

Zeaxanthin

Zeaxanthin is another carotenoid concentrated in the macula. It works alongside lutein to help filter certain high-energy wavelengths of light and protect delicate eye tissues from oxidative damage. Adequate intake of these carotenoids is important for maintaining healthy macular function.
🍁

Vitamin A

Vitamin A is an essential nutrient necessary for normal vision, especially in low-light conditions. It helps the eyes produce rhodopsin, a pigment required for seeing in dim light, and supports the normal maintenance of the cornea and other eye tissues.
🍁

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a water-soluble antioxidant that helps protect cells against oxidative stress. It also contributes to collagen formation, which supports the normal structure of tissues throughout the body, including structures within the eye. Adequate vitamin C intake is an important part of balanced nutritional support for eye health.
🍁

Vitamin E

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant that helps protect cell membranes from oxidative damage. In eye-health formulas, it complements other antioxidants and nutrients that support normal cellular function and help maintain healthy eye tissues.
🍁

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral involved in many biological processes, including normal immune function and vitamin A metabolism. It plays an important role in transporting vitamin A from the liver to the retina, helping support normal vision and retinal function.
🍁

Copper

Copper is an essential trace mineral that contributes to normal connective-tissue formation, iron metabolism, and antioxidant enzyme activity. It is sometimes included alongside zinc in eye-health supplements to help maintain an appropriate balance of these minerals.
🍁

Bilberry Extract

Bilberry is a fruit rich in naturally occurring anthocyanins, which are plant compounds with antioxidant properties. It is traditionally associated with eye wellness and is commonly included in vision-support formulas. However, evidence that bilberry supplements improve night vision or correct eyesight remains limited.
🍁

Ginkgo Biloba

Ginkgo biloba is a botanical extract traditionally used in herbal supplements. It contains plant compounds that have been studied for their antioxidant properties and potential effects on circulation. Its inclusion in an eye-health formula is intended to complement overall wellness, although evidence for specific vision benefits is not conclusive.
🍁

Turmeric Extract

Turmeric contains curcumin and other plant compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is used in various nutritional supplements to support the body's normal response to oxidative stress. Its specific benefits for eyesight, however, require further clinical research.
🍁

Saffron Extract

Saffron is a botanical containing compounds such as crocin and crocetin. These compounds have been studied for their antioxidant properties and potential role in supporting retinal function. Saffron is sometimes included in vision-support formulas, but its effects depend on the dose, preparation, and individual circumstances.
🍁

Black Pepper Extract

Black pepper extract contains piperine, a compound that can influence the absorption and metabolism of certain substances. It is sometimes added to supplement formulations to improve the bioavailability of selected ingredients. Its presence and suitability should be confirmed on the product's current label.
